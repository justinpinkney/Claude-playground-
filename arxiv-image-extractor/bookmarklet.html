arXiv Image Extractor Bookmarklet

Click on any arXiv page (abstract, PDF, or HTML) and instantly view all images from the paper in a grid overlay.

Drag this link to your bookmarks bar:

arXiv Images

Installation Instructions

  1. Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (press Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows/Linux or Cmd+Shift+B on Mac)
  2. Drag the "arXiv Images" link above to your bookmarks bar
  3. That's it! The bookmarklet is installed.

How to Use

  1. Navigate to any arXiv page:
    • https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21986 (abstract page)
    • https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.21986 (PDF viewer)
    • https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21986v1 (HTML page)
  2. Click the "arXiv Images" bookmark in your bookmarks bar
  3. A full-screen overlay will appear showing all images from the paper in a grid
  4. Click "Close" or press Escape to close the overlay
Note: The bookmarklet extracts images from the HTML version of arXiv papers. If a paper doesn't have an HTML version, no images will be found.

Features

  • Works on abstract, PDF, and HTML arXiv pages
  • Displays images in a responsive grid
  • Open or download individual images
  • Automatic CORS proxy fallback
  • Minimalist brutalist design
  • No installation required - just drag and drop
